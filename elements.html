Level 4 and 5 Higher National Diploma in Business (UK)

Level 4 and 5 Higher National Diploma in Business is about to equip learners with business skills, knowledge and the understanding necessary to achieve high performance in the global business environment. Also it provides insight and understanding into international business operations and the opprtunities and challenges business operations and the opportunities and chalenges presented by a globalised market place. Furthermore, it offers a balance between employability skills and the knowledge essential for students with entrepreneurial, employment or academic aspirations.
